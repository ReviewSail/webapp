# ReviewSail Marketing Website Concept & Feature Blueprint

This document outlines the core concept, key features, and page structure of the ReviewSail marketing website to serve as a conceptual blueprint for Figma wireframing and design.

---

## 1. Core Brand Concept & Value Proposition

ReviewSail is an **automated guest feedback and review-acceleration engine** designed specifically for hospitality providers, vacation rental hosts (Airbnb, VRBO), boutique hotels, and physical service locations.

### The Problem it Solves (The Hook)
*   **The Review Gap:** Happy guests rarely leave reviews, while unhappy guests leave negative public ratings on Google Maps.
*   **The Solution:** ReviewSail intercepts guest feedback by giving them a private, direct channel to share comments with management first. If they are highly satisfied, it directs them to post publicly on Google Maps.

---

## 2. Recommended Landing Page Structure (Figma Wireframe Base)

A high-converting single-page layout structure consists of the following conceptual sections:

### A. The Hero Section (Above the Fold)
*   **Core Headline Idea:** "Skyrocket Your Google Reviews, Automate Guest Feedback."
*   **Supporting Sub-headline:** "Connect ReviewSail with your checkout workflow. Automatically send friendly email and SMS invites that drive public Google stars while capturing private complaints directly."
*   **Primary Call-to-Action (CTA):** "Start Free Trial" (Redirects to App Sign-Up).
*   **Visual Asset Placeholder:** A side-by-side mockup of the desktop dashboard alongside a smartphone screen receiving an SMS invite.

### B. The "Dual-Channel Routing" Concept (The Core Mechanic)
This section should visually demonstrate how the system handles different guest ratings:
1.  **Guest rates stay 4-5 Stars:** The system prompts them with a highly visible "Share publicly on Google" CTA, driving direct public review growth.
2.  **Guest rates stay 1-3 Stars:** The system presents a private form sending feedback directly and confidentially to property management—enabling service recovery before negative feedback hits the internet.

### C. Key Platform Features Grid
Designers should allocate feature blocks or card grids for the following highlighted capabilities:
*   **Hands-Free Syncing:** Easily import CSV checkout reports from Airbnb, Booking.com, or Expedia to queue review requests automatically.
*   **Multi-Channel Reach:** Reach guests where they look—choose automated email (powered by Resend) or direct SMS invites (powered by Twilio).
*   **Preferred Send Scheduling:** Define custom times (e.g., 10:00 AM after checkout) to trigger requests when guests are most responsive.
*   **Unsubscribe & Compliance:** Built-in proactive suppression and self-managed unsubscribe links protect user inbox preferences automatically.
*   **Collaborative Property Management:** Grant specialized "Admin" or "Staff" access privileges to on-site employees or managers.

### D. The Interactive Mockup Walkthrough
Create an interactive visual component in Figma modeling a real customer experience:
*   **Step 1:** Guest checks out and receives a clean, personalized text or email.
*   **Step 2:** Guest clicks, selects a star rating, and leaves a private comment.
*   **Step 3 (Success):** Guest is given a direct link to post on Google Maps or gets a warm thank-you from management.

### E. Transparent Pricing Tier
Showcase a single, compelling tier to drive conversions:
*   **Premium Pro Plan — $49/mo**
*   **Key Offerings Included:**
    *   Unlimited property locations.
    *   Automated multi-channel Email & SMS invitations.
    *   Built-in private feedback manager & direct team portal.
    *   No-setup background automated job scheduler.
    *   Start with a risk-free trial.

## 3. Key User Journeys for Interactive Prototyping

When mapping out clickable flows in Figma, prioritize these two main routes:
1.  **The Conversion Funnel:** Visitor scans the value propositions -> clicks "Start Free Trial" -> lands on the clean registration/login screen.
2.  **The Guest Experience Flow:** A micro-prototype demonstrating what a guest sees when they receive a review request link, submit a rating, and get redirected.
