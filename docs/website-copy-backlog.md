# ReviewSail website updates for the pilot

Draft for the marketing site after the webapp pilot is live.

**Homepage headline**

Give guests a way to ask for help as soon as they arrive.

**Supporting copy**

Share a guest help link before arrival or print it as a QR code. Your team sees the message in one inbox and can follow up while the guest is still at the property.

**How it works**

1. Set your property’s usual arrival time and choose when to check in.
2. Add guests or share the property QR code in your arrival instructions.
3. Open a guest report, contact the guest, and mark the issue resolved when you’ve handled it.

**FAQ copy**

**How soon can guests report an issue?** Guests can open the help link immediately, including before a scheduled message arrives. They can write directly to the property team without an account or a star rating.

**When do check-ins go out?** With messaging enabled, new properties start with an arrival check-in and a Day 2 follow-up. The arrival check-in becomes eligible two hours after expected arrival. The follow-up becomes eligible at noon the next day on longer stays. Hosts can choose just one or turn both off. Quiet hours, departure dates, and the sending schedule affect delivery.

**Does reporting a problem affect the Google review invite?** Guests who report an issue through their booking link remain eligible for the same post-checkout invitation. Public reviews are optional.

**Will the host see my message immediately?** A confirmed submission is available in the property’s guest inbox. Team response times vary. Owners can enable alerts to a verified email address.
